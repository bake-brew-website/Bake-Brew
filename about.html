Our Story

At Bake & Brew, we believe in more than just food. We believe in a story – a story of warmth, of passion, and of bringing people together. From our humble beginnings in a tiny kitchen to becoming a beloved part of the community, every moment has been fueled by a love for great food and shared experiences.

Meet the Founder

What started as a passion project in a tiny kitchen has grown into a beloved community cafe. Our founder, Ashish, brings his heritage, creativity, and values into every pastry and every interaction, making Bake & Brew more than just a place to eat – it’s a place to feel at home.
